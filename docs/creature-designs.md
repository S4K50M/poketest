# Supplied creature design reference

1. Pyrojaw (Fire/Rock) – The Heavy-Armor Brawler/Gator (Quadrupedal)
   - Design: An imposing, quadrupedal volcan-alligator with jagged, dark obsidian plates on its back and glowing orange magma seams along its scales. It has a heavy, igneous rock club on the end of its burning tail and a fiery, volcanic snout.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Runs on all fours with heavy, earth-shaking thuds. It bites with aggressive lunges and swings its heavy, smoldering tail-club.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Magma Burp): Launches short-range, explosive volcanic rocks that create burning floor hazards.
     - Forward + L (Igneous Rush): A heavy, armored forward charge on all fours, shrugging off light attacks.
     - Special Movement: Tail-propelling thrust or ground-shake slam.
2. Voltantis (Electric/Bug) – The Speedster Scythe-Insect (Winged/Levitating)
   - Design: A hovering, yellow-and-black insect resembling a hybrid of a praying mantis and wasp. Features massive, translucent buzzing wings, long spark-generating antennae, and natural forelimb blades made of crackling, translucent yellow plasma.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Hovering above the ground, it zigzags rapidly. Combats with lightning-fast, blurring arm-scythe swipes and aerial strikes.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Spark Blitz): Shoots rapid, homing electric needles that lightly stun enemies.
     - Directional + L (Plasma Dash): A near-instantaneous teleport-dash in any direction, leaving a damaging electric trail.
3. Glaciolith (Ice/Ground) – The Mighty Grappling Yeti (Bipedal/Bulky Beast)
   - Design: A hulking, bipedal yeti-like beast covered in shaggy white-and-pale-blue fur. Heavily armored in jagged, natural glacier-ice blocks on its giant forearms and shoulders, sporting curled blue icy horns.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Lumbering, heavy strides. Attacks with giant, icy double-hand slams, swipes, and aggressive forward tackles.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Glacier Toss): Pulls an ice-boulder from the ground and hurls it.
     - Forward + L (Permafrost Grab): Lurches forward to grab, slamming opponents into ice spikes that freeze/slow their dash speed.
4. Zephyrix (Flying/Fairy) – The Swift Aerial Guardian (Winged Quadruped)
   - Design: A graceful, avian griffin-like winged quadruped with soft teal and white plumage. It features majestic, cloud-tipped wings, a sharp beak, and a swirling crest/mane of puffy, air-current-like feathers. No hands—just talons.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Aloft or hovering, performing swift beak jabs, aerial talons, and gust-generating wing-slams. Can fly and air-dash freely.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Gale Torrent): Shoots a swirling wind-cyclone that pulls in smaller enemies/minions and sweeps them up.
     - Directional + L (Aero-Dive): Swoops forward in an air-shield, knocking back grounded targets.
5. Terrakhan (Earth/Bug) – The Indestructible Fortress (6-Legged Beetloid)
   - Design: A heavily armored, low-slung, six-legged beast hybrid of a rhino and a stag beetle. It wears dark earth-brown and mossy-green natural armor plates, featuring a massive, spiral-patterned rock horn on its forehead.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Tank-like scuttling. It rams with its massive horn, stomps with armored legs, and executes heavy body checks.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Fissure Stamp): Ground-pound that triggers a linear erupting rock-wave.
     - Hold + L (Fortress Shell): Hunkers down to auto-block and absorb frontal attacks, unleashing a shockwave when released.
6. Noxshade (Dark/Ghost) – The Ghostly Mirage (Quadrupedal Quad-beast)
   - Design: A sleek, quadrupedal shadow-fox/jackal with dark purple and black fur, glowing violet eyes, and a massive, wispy tail made of ghostly purple shadow-flame.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Low, evasive stalking. It moves like a blur, attacking with ghostly bites, shadow claw-swipes, and phantom-tail sweeps.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Shadow Mirage): Leaves a decoy copy that explodes into blinding dark mist when hit.
     - Directional + L (Prowler Fade): Melts into the ground as a shadow, reappearing elsewhere to surprise-strike from behind.
7. Aqualance (Water/Steel) – The armored Spindle-Shark (Bipedal Water-beast)
   - Design: A bipedal, armor-plated shark-like creature with sleek deep-blue and metallic-silver scales. Hammerhead-inspired eyes with a sharp, spiraled, steel javelin-like "nose" lance, plus razor-sharp dorsal and arm fins.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Fluid, slide-dashing "swimming" motions on land. Attacks with rapid lance-jabs, spinning fin-slams, and lunges.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Hydro Jet): Shoots a high-pressure, piercing jet-stream that pushes targets away and wall-splats them.
     - Forward + L (Spiral Piercer): A spinning forward drill-rush using its javelin nose.
8. Ferrodrake (Steel/Dragon) – The Metallic Dragon (Bipedal Winged)
   - Design: A bipedal, armored dragon covered in chrome and crimson steel scales. It has angular, metallic wings, exhaust-vent shoulders, and a spiky tail.
   - Move/Combat Animations: Heavy, stomping movement on foot, punctuated by quick wing-assisted leaps and thrusts. Fights with claw-slashes, tail-swipes, and headbutts.
   - Skills (L Specials):
     - Neutral + L (Steel-Fire Breath): Exhausts a searing barrage of metallic embers/sparks in a cone.
     - Directional + L (Aegis Wing): Feathers of steel flare to guard, parrying/reversing incoming heavy moves.
9. Sinder (Fire) – The Spark Lizard (Tiny Bipedal)
   - Visual & Movement: Tiny, hyperactive bipedal orange fire-lizard with a small, flickering tail-tip flame. It scampers fast, leaping to bite.
   - Brawler Function: Swarms in 3s. Shoots tiny, annoying spark-projectiles to interrupt inputs or pounces on back/legs, dealing minor burn chip-damage.
10. Voltlet (Electric/Bug) – The Static Grub (Tiny Segmented)
    - Visual & Movement: Chubby, yellow-and-black larval grub with sparking, tiny antennae. It crawls and bounces, vibrating with static.
    - Brawler Function: Explodes on touch/attack with static sparks, applying 1 second of paralysis/slowness to players who don't space their attacks.
11. Pebbleback (Rock/Ground) – The Rock Tortoise (Tiny Quadrupedal)
    - Visual & Movement: A small, slow-moving, heavily armored tortoise with a rough rock shell.
    - Brawler Function: Highly resistant to standard J (Quick Attack) strikes. Requires a heavy K (Impact) move to flip them, breaking their guard. Block and bottleneck players.
12. Dewdrop (Water) – The Bubble Tadpole (Tiny Aquatic/Levitating)
    - Visual & Movement: A bouncy, translucent-blue tadpole with big eyes and tiny fins, gliding/splashing around on wet surfaces.
    - Brawler Function: Spits slippery bubble traps on the floor that cause the player to slide/lose traction during quick movement.
13. Sproutlet (Grass/Dark) – The Thorny Bulb (Tiny Root-beast)
    - Visual & Movement: A tiny, mischievous bulb-like plant with leafy arms and glowing dark eyes, scurrying on root-like feet.
    - Brawler Function: Burrows into the ground to become invisible, popping up to whip players with thorny vines or trap their feet (immobilizing them for a split second).
