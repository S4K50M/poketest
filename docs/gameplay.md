# Gameplay and controls

[Documentation index](README.md) · [Project README](../README.md)

## First expedition

Choose three distinct companions and begin at the Last Lantern. Move toward Professor Vell, use E to read the opening dialogue, and enter the Mourningwild portal. Restore guardian fragments to open the next chapter. Benches restore the party and establish checkpoints. M opens the atlas; N opens quests, talents, equipment, bonds, covenants, and history.

| 1 / 2 / 3, or Q | Switch among the three active companions |
| E | Talk, rest, read, open a cache, pull a lever, enter a door |
| U | Grapple toward a nearby golden anchor after unlocking Ember Thread |
| T / C | Use / cycle the Resonance Pick and Echo Lantern |
| B | Buy a party-wide Heartwood upgrade near a waylight |

| R | Threefold Resonance Surge, when its gauge reaches 100% |

Mobile gameplay is landscape-only. Portrait pauses the adventure and shows a rotate prompt; the home screen still supports portrait. Movement/aim is on the left; strike, jump, dash, Guard, heavy, special, and power are on the right. The left-pad SWAP button or party cards switch companions. Hold-to-heal and nearby interaction buttons sit beside movement; grapple appears after its unlock. The menu prioritizes recovery and loadouts, then exploration and tools. Keyboard controls are also listed in the in-game guide. Sound effects are optional and start muted.

## Party and survival

Each companion keeps independent hearts, Focus, and cooldowns. Switching preserves location, velocity, debuffs, and spent air resources. It cannot refill jumps or grant free invulnerability. A knocked-out companion yields to a living partner. A full party wipe returns to the checkpoint, resets surviving guardians to full health, and loses 10% of Lumens. Resting revives the party.

The eight supplied identities retain their different anatomy and elemental skills: the quadrupedal magma gator, mantis-wasp, glacier yeti, cloud griffin, rock beetle, shadow jackal, natural nose-lance shark, and chrome dragon. The new companions use separate deer, moth, and owl rigs:

- **Mycora:** fungal antlers, moss cloak, healing seed communion, and an armored briar rush.
- **Lumivern:** four moon-patterned wings, a lantern heart, seeking light needles, and a solar cone.
- **Chronowl:** six feathered wings, an hourglass crown, a stasis ring, and an ice dash.

Minions include shell enemies that need a heavy hit, static grubs that punish close attacks, burrowing ambushers, slippery water creatures, ranged thorn sprites, furnace bats, ink rays, storm birds, frost crawlers, and teleporting rift echoes. Guardians telegraph attacks and change rhythm below half health. Their attacks combine charges, jumpable waves, root eruptions, aimed ink, lightning markers, slowing ice, and projectile fans.

## Inventory and crafting

Defeating creatures and finding echoes awards Bond XP and Lumens. Bond levels improve party health and damage. Caches and ore provide regional materials; the Resonance Pick breaks ore and the Echo Lantern reveals hidden shrine rewards. Rewards persist across room changes and cannot be collected twice.

Craft flasks and four charms at benches or the Mender’s Workbench. Equip two charms: Feral Fang improves damage, Wellspring improves Focus regeneration, Swift Feather improves running and grapple range, and Briar Heart retaliates when hit. Heartwood forging offers three party-wide upgrades. Companions can be replaced only near a bench or workbench.

The original campaign save records party health, progression, inventory, charms, discoveries, claimed rewards, defeated creatures, story notes, endings, and a room checkpoint. Reloading starts at that checkpoint; it does not preserve the exact midair position or a guardian’s partial health. Autosaves happen during play and at room changes, rewards, resting, crafting, and leaving. An older arena save is not used by the new campaign.

## RPG progression

Bond XP improves party level, health, and damage. Two starting talent points let you begin a Fang, Heart, or Thread build. Additional points come from Bond levels, forbidden guardians, and cleared world events. Talents affect damage, execution, lifesteal, healing, inactive Focus recovery, survival, movement, loot collection, and synergy.

RPG equipment is separate from crafted charms: the party can equip **one relic and one armor item**, alongside **two crafted charms**. Relics and armor modify damage, defense, speed, Focus recovery, or Surge gain. Unequipped equipment can be salvaged once for Lumens. Ora sells finite stock at the sanctuary; purchases are not a renewable item farm.

Individual companion mastery rises through that companion’s attacks and kills. Ranks occur at 80, 200, and 400 mastery: Kindred, Soulbound, and Awakened. They add damage and visual auras. Reaching 200 on any companion also unlocks Kindred Choir in the Arsenal.

Direct hits charge Threefold Resonance. At 100%, R calls a protected multi-companion burst that restores Focus to living party members. Its own damage cannot immediately refill the entire gauge.

Talk to Iona at the sanctuary to begin the optional chronicle. Speak to quest givers to accept objectives and turn in ready quests; N lets you track one. World History collects revealed history and recovered field notes. The three covenant choices are permanent for that expedition; see the [world guide](world-and-story.md).

After fifteen seconds of simulation time in a region path, an uncleared world fracture can appear. Defeat its three echoes for Lumens, a talent point, and a flask. Completed events persist; leaving a partial event does not restore its defeated enemies for farming.

## Accessibility and presentation

Use H → Arsenal to toggle reduced motion. It removes camera shake and softens flashes; a new journey initially follows the browser’s motion preference. The sound button toggles optional synthesized effects. Pausing, opening a menu, losing window focus, or hiding the tab stops active gameplay through the UI.

For exact timing and art unlocks, see the [combat reference](combat.md). For checkpoint and storage limitations, see [save format](save-format.md).
